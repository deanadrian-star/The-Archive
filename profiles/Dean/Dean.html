The first record documents the discovery of a peculiar stone buried beneath the ruins of an abandoned sanctuary. Its surface was cold to the touch, yet faintly illuminated by an unfamiliar light. Strange symbols covered its surface, arranged in patterns that no known language could decipher. Those who first examined it reported hearing distant voices, even when the chamber stood completely silent.

It was believed to be nothing more than an ancient artifact. That belief did not last. The stone had been dormant for centuries, and its awakening marked the beginning of something far more sinister.

The second record explores the peculiar properties of the recovered stones. Their markings appear to shift when observed for prolonged periods, while certain fragments respond to nearby objects and even the presence of living beings. Attempts to translate their inscriptions have yielded incomplete results, revealing only fragments of a much older and more troubling history.

Yet the most unsettling discovery was not their strange properties, but their apparent connection to one another. Each stone seems to form part of a greater design, as though they were once pieces of a single, forgotten creation.

At last, we arrive at the final record. The deepest chamber of the vault contains the oldest and most dangerous stones ever recovered. Unlike the others, these relics remain bound within elaborate seals, their surfaces scarred by markings that seem to resist even the slightest examination.

Their purpose remains unknown. Perhaps they were created to contain something, or perhaps they were the source of the very curse that now surrounds them. Whatever the truth, the seals must remain untouched.
